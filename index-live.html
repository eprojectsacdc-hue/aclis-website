About ACLIS

Engineering & Advisory

Explore our expertise →

ACLIS is an independent engineering and advisory practice supporting energy infrastructure, renewable energy, battery storage and sustainable mobility projects.

We provide technical expertise, engineering support and strategic advisory services to developers, investors, EPC contractors, utilities and asset owners throughout the project lifecycle.

What we do

Integrated Engineering Solutions

Independent technical expertise across the energy project lifecycle.

Power Systems

Grid connection advisory, substations, power system studies and grid engineering.

Renewable Energy

Solar, wind and hybrid projects from feasibility through detailed engineering.

BESS Solutions

Battery energy storage systems engineering, grid integration and technical advisory.

E-Mobility

EV charging infrastructure design and integrated solutions for electric mobility.

Technical Due Diligence

Independent technical assessments, risk evaluation and engineering reports supporting investment and financing decisions.

Electrical Design

Electrical design for LV, MV and HV systems, electrical installations and energy infrastructure.

Agrivoltaic Development

Integrated agrivoltaic solutions for renewable energy generation and efficient agricultural land use.

Knowledge & Training

Technical training, workshops and professional development programs.
